Official B.Pharm Major Project | Final Year 2025–2026 Scopus Indexed Research

Development and Pharmaceutical Evaluation of Cosmetic Serum and Toner Incorporating Pineapple Peel Extract

Conducted at Kamalakshi Pandurangan College of Pharmacy | Affiliated to The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University

Published in World Journal of Pharmaceutical Research (WJPR) | Volume 15, Issue 3, 2026

Read WJPR Paper

This academic B.Pharm dissertation research explored circular green pharmacy—extracting active proteolytic bromelain enzymes and therapeutic polyphenols from agro-waste pineapple peels (Ananas comosus). The isolated bioactives were formulated into two cosmeceutical dosage forms (serum and toner) and evaluated for physicochemical stability, viscosity, antimicrobial potency, and skin compatibility according to Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP) and ICH Q1A guidelines.
